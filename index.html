Why use it

  • Nothing is locked. Many tracker apps log for free and charge for more routines, older charts or small tools like a plate calculator. Setstamp has no paid plan.
  • Works with no signal. Every set is written to your phone first and syncs when you're back online.
  • Quick between sets. Each set is filled in from the one above or from last session; tap the check to log it and the rest timer starts.

Compared with Strong and Hevy

Both let you log workouts for free. At the time of writing (November 2026), reviews citing each app's documentation report these free-plan limits:

  • Saved routines: Setstamp unlimited; Strong 3 templates; Hevy 4 routines.
  • Custom exercises: Setstamp unlimited; Hevy 7.
  • Progress chart history: Setstamp all of it; Strong some charts need Pro; Hevy about 3 months.
  • Plate calculator: Setstamp included; Strong Pro; Hevy included.
  • Warm-up sets: Setstamp included; Strong and Hevy Pro.
  • Paid plan: Setstamp none; Strong $23.99 to $29.99 a year; Hevy $23.99 a year.
  • App store apps and Apple Watch: Strong and Hevy yes; Setstamp is a web app.

What's in the free account

  • Programs: upper/lower, push/pull/legs and full-body templates, or build your own days.
  • Supersets, and weight with reps, time or distance, plus bodyweight and assisted exercises.
  • 1,383 exercises with on-device search that handles typos and nicknames. You can add your own.
  • Plates per side, warm-up sets, a rest timer and progression hints that show their rule.
  • Stats: records, volume and a training calendar over your full history, and estimated weekly sets per muscle on a body map.
  • Your data: export everything as JSON or your sets as CSV, or delete your account and its data.

Install it on your phone

Setstamp is a web app. Create an account in your browser, then add it to your home screen: in Safari on iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen; in Chrome on Android, open the menu and choose Install app.

Questions

Is it really free? Yes. There's no paid plan, no trial and no ads.

Does it work offline? Yes, once you've signed in on that phone. Sets save on the device and sync when you're back online.

Can I import from Strong or Hevy? No. There's no import.
