A free lifting log that works offline

Log weight and reps with one hand between sets. It works without signal, keeps your full history, and has no subscription.

Create free account or sign in.

Free, with no limits on history, programs or exercises. You can export or delete your data any time.

How it works

  1. Open: Today shows the next day of your program. A missed session stays next in line.
  2. Log: weight and reps are prefilled from last time. Tap Log set and the rest timer starts.
  3. See progress: personal records, estimated 1RM and weekly sets per muscle.

What's included

  • Works offline. Sets save to your phone first and sync when you're back online.
  • One-handed logging with large numbers and big plus and minus buttons.
  • 1,383 exercises with on-device search that handles typos and nicknames. You can add your own.
  • Programs: upper/lower, push/pull/legs and full-body templates, or build your own.
  • Body-map coverage: estimated weekly sets per muscle.
  • Your data: export everything as JSON or CSV, or delete your account and its data.

Install it on your phone

Create an account, then add Setstamp to your home screen from your browser. No app store needed. It opens full screen from its own icon and works without signal.
