Privacy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Setstamp is a free lifting log. It has no ads and no analytics, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing. This page lists what the app stores and where.

What we store

  • Your account: email address and a hashed password. If you sign in with Google, we also store the name, email and profile picture URL that Google shares.
  • Your training data: workouts, sets, programs, custom exercises, favorites and settings.
  • Sign-in sessions, so you stay signed in.

Where it lives

  • On your device, in the browser's storage, so the app works offline.
  • On Convex, the database that syncs your data between devices. It is hosted in Sydney, Australia (ap-southeast-2).
  • The website is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address when you load it.

Email

Account emails (confirming your address, resetting your password, security notices) are sent through a Gmail account run by the developer. We don't send marketing email.

Exercise images

Some exercise pictures load from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), so GitHub sees your IP address when they load. You can turn off community images in Settings.

Export and delete

In Settings you can export everything as JSON or CSV, delete all your training data, or delete your account. Deleting your account removes your data from our database. When you sign out, you can also clear the copy stored on that device.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/Koala3353/setstamp.
